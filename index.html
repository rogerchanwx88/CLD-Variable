Check your variable names

A variable should be a neutral noun or noun phrase describing something that can meaningfully increase or decrease over time. This tool does not judge causal links or your story.

Variable naming feedback

Checks run entirely in your browser. No information is uploaded. This is a rule-based learning aid, not generative AI. A green tick means the name passed the available naming rules, not that the student's narrative has been assessed.
